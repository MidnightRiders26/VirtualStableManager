Market & Pricing

Market references and pricing guidance for the breeds in your stable, expressed in Horse Reality Credits (HRC).

Market Overview

Lowest and highest suggested Buyout Prices and Stud Fees for each breed, based on the values available in your stable.

Breed market summaries will appear here when horse records are available.

Background Market References

Informational price ranges only. These are not automatic valuations and are not assigned to individual horses.

APPALOOSA
LOWER-END MARKET

Companion & Project Horses

500–2,500 HRC

Older stock, companions, and green projects.

YOUNG STOCK

Weanlings & Yearlings

2,000–10,000 HRC

Unstarted young horses, with value shaped by quality and bloodlines.

RIDING MARKET

Trail & Pleasure Horses

3,500–16,500 HRC

Started or experienced riding horses, generally ages 3 to 15+.

PERFORMANCE MARKET

Show & Specialized Horses

15,000–45,000+ HRC

Competitive horses, specialized disciplines, and quality broodmares.

Appaloosa Stud Fee Benchmarks

The same evaluated fee applies to live cover and each semen vial.

Entry-Level & Foundation Younger prospects and regional foundation stallions
400–750 HRC
Standard Show & Proven Established stallions with show records or proven breeding value
850–1,200 HRC
Elite & Futurity Elite money-earners, high-level performers, and standout sires
1,500+ HRC
Vial pricing rule

Each semen vial uses the stallion's evaluated stud fee—the same price as live cover. The 200 HRC collection cost is tracked separately as an expense when calculating vial profitability.

Pricing Reference Factors

Considerations used when evaluating an individual horse's overall market and breeding value.

  • Genetic Potential & Conformation Overall quality and suitability for the horse's intended discipline.
  • Training & Performance Level of training, competition history, results, and earnings.
  • Bloodlines & Genetics Pedigree, useful inherited traits, and breeding relevance.
  • Offspring Record Quality and proven performance of offspring, where applicable.
  • Health, Fertility, Age & COI Soundness, reproductive value, age, and responsible breeding considerations.
  • Marketability Demand for the horse's qualities and intended use.

Market & Pricing Notes

How pricing estimates are intended to be used in VSM

Suggested pricing

VSM uses available horse information to help estimate reasonable pricing. Suggestions are not guaranteed sale values.

Starting Bid

A suggested minimum opening bid based on the horse's overall value.

Buyout Price

A suggested price representing the horse's estimated full market worth.

Stud Fee

A suggested breeding fee based on the stallion's overall breeding value, not GP alone.
